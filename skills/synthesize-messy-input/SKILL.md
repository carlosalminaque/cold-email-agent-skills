# Synthesize messy input

Needs: none

Some people hand over a lot of raw material at once and expect the agent to do the thinking they have not spelled out. Answering only the literal question undersells what they asked for.

## Steps

1. **Read everything before answering anything.** Every upload, every link, every screenshot, in full. A synthesis built on a sample is worse than none.
2. **Name the real question.** Restate what they are actually asking in one or two lines, so they can correct the framing early.
3. **Judge fit against what they already have.** Before saying whether something is useful, read their current files, tools or process it would touch. Never judge fit from memory or assumption.
4. **Answer the next questions too.** What is usable, what is hype, how it would actually be put in place, and what it changes in their current setup.
5. **Separate hype from substance.** Flag self-reported numbers, small samples and promotional framing. Still credit a sound idea on its own merits.
6. **End with a recommendation.** What to do first, second and third, and what happens if they say go. Do not build it unasked.
7. **Thorough, not a wall of text.** Take the space it needs, in short labelled sections.

Valid: "You're really asking whether this replaces your current reply process. It doesn't: two of its five ideas fit, here is why, do X first." Invalid: a summary of each upload with no answer.
