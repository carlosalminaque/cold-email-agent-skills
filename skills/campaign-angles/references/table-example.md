# Example angles table

A fictional table for an outbound service selling to small B2B software companies. Company names and numbers are invented. Your own filters come from your one source.

| # | Tier | List filters | Angle | Per-account research? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Broad | B2B software, 10 to 50 staff, your countries | Cold email running without hiring for it | No (fixed text per segment) |
| 2 | Broad | Same, with a public pricing page | Sales-led pricing, few sales staff | Light |
| 3 | Broad | Same, with a careers page | Hiring in product, not in sales | Yes |
| 4 | Focused | Broad + funding round in last 12 months | New money, sales not yet staffed | Yes |
| 5 | Focused | Broad + first sales hire posted | Replies coming in before the hire starts | Yes |
| 6 | Focused | Broad + new sales leader on the team page | Fill the calendar while they build | Yes |
| 7 | Niche | Funding + first sales post open | Two dated triggers, same quarter | Deep |
| 8 | Niche | Launch + funding + no sales titles | Launched, funded, no outbound | Deep |

Front-end offers:

- "Want two sample emails written for your market?"
- "Want a short list of 20 named accounts that fit your best customer?"

Order is always Broad, then Focused, then Niche. A real table has 15 to 20 rows.
